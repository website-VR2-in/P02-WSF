FAQ

Does it read my whole inbox?

No. ClawTool processes exactly what you paste or drop — nothing more. There is no inbox access to grant, because there is nothing to grant.

What happens to my data?

It's processed in the EU to extract the tasks and then discarded, by default. Pro can opt into 90-day history. We never train shared models on your content.

How accurate is the extraction?

Every item carries a confidence note and the source line, so you verify in seconds rather than trust blindly. In testing on real inboxes, over 90% of tasks are extracted with the right owner and deadline.

Which tools does it sync to?

Todoist, Notion, Slack and email today. Markdown copy always. More connectors ship as the most-requested ones — vote on the roadmap via the contact form.
