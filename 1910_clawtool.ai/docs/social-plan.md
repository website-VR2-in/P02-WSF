# 1910 clawtool.ai — social channel plan (step 6)

## Recommended channels (priority)
1. **X / Twitter** — AI product launch lives here; build-in-public, screenshots of real extractions.
2. **Product Hunt** — the launch itself (coordinate with product go-live, not the marketing site).
3. **LinkedIn** — secondary, for the team/enterprise angle.
4. **Hacker News** — Show HN at product launch.

## First 30 days (pre-product)
- Day 1: "We're building a button for the 200-email problem." — positioning post.
- Day 5: screenshot of a real thread → tasks mock (anonymized), "what should this extract?" — engagement.
- Day 12: "Zero-retention by default: why that's the only sane default for a tool that reads your work." — values post.
- Day 20: pricing reveal (€0 / €19 / €49) + waitlist CTA.
- Day 30: product alpha invite post (if ready).

## KPIs
- X: 1,000 followers, 100 waitlist signups by launch.
- Product Hunt: top 5 of the day at launch.
- Site: waitlist ≥ 300 before GA.

## Tone
- Product voice: show the extraction, show the diff, no hype adjectives.
