FAQ

Will my prospects know it's AI?

They'll notice the messages are more relevant, not that they're automated. Every sequence is reviewable, and you can switch any touchpoint to manual. We never impersonate a human when asked — disclosure is configured per campaign.

Is my data used to train other customers' models?

No. Your corpus (accounts, voices, replies) is isolated per tenant. Shared-model training is off by default and requires an explicit, per-feature opt-in you can revoke.

What about deliverability and GDPR?

Sender-reputation guardrails (warm-up, volume caps, bounce suppression), one-click opt-outs, EU data residency and a signed DPA on every plan. We treat your domain like it's our own.

How fast do we see meetings?

Pilot teams typically get their first qualified meeting within the first two weeks of a 14-day pilot, once the initial ICP is validated. If the first wave underperforms, we rebuild the ICP with you — free.
