# 1907 salesclawai.com — social channel plan (step 6)

## Recommended channels (in priority order)
1. **LinkedIn (company page + founder)** — B2B sales audience lives here; highest conversion channel.
   - Cadence: 3 posts/week (1 product insight, 1 use-case story, 1 customer/pilot result).
   - Founder posts convert better than company posts for a pre-PMF SaaS.
2. **X / Twitter** — AI-tool audience; product demos + thread on "how AI SDRs work".
   - Cadence: 2–3/week + 1 demo video every 2 weeks.
3. **YouTube (later)** — 1 Loom-style walkthrough per month once the product is stable.

## First 30 days
- Day 1: launch post ("We built an AI sales agent — free 14-day pilot") on LinkedIn + X.
- Day 3: demo GIF (ICP → meeting in calendar).
- Day 7: "5 signs your outbound is broken" (value post).
- Day 14: first pilot result numbers (with permission).
- Day 30: case study post.

## KPIs
- LinkedIn: 100 followers month 1, 5 qualified DMs/week.
- X: 200 followers month 1.
- Site: social-referral sessions ≥ 20% of non-organic traffic by month 2.
