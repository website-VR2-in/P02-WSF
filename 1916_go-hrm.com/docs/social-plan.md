# Social Plan — Go HRM (launch phase)

## Positioning
B2B HR SaaS. Audience: HR directors, founders/ops leads of 10–500 person companies, HR consultancies. Tone: professional but human, French-first, some English content for EU reach.

## Channels (priority order)
1. **LinkedIn (company page + founder personal profile)** — primary. HR buyers live here.
2. **X** — secondary; product updates + HR-tech chatter.
3. **Newsletter** — later, once there is cadence.

## Content pillars (weekly mix)
- **Pain → fix** (2 posts/wk): "Your payroll is still in Excel" → demo of the payroll module.
- **Product** (1–2): new module detail, migration story, pricing clarity.
- **HR insight** (1): GDPR tips, leave-management best practices, hiring metrics.
- **Social proof** (as available): beta quotes, case numbers.

## Cadence
- 3–4 LinkedIn posts/week (2 company, 1–2 founder profile).
- 1 X thread/week repurposing the LinkedIn post.
- First 2 weeks: 1 post/day to seed the page (announcements, team, values, GDPR angle).

## Launch week script
- Day 1: "We launched Go HRM — the HR suite built for SMEs" + link `/fr/`.
- Day 2: pain post (Excel payroll).
- Day 3: GDPR/EU hosting angle.
- Day 4: pricing transparency post.
- Day 5: 15-min demo offer, founder profile.
- Days 6–7: reply to every comment, DM warm prospects.

## Cross-promotion
- ESA / HCEA ecosystem (1904/1905): one LinkedIn announcement each.
- Claw AI family (1907–1912) sites: no natural fit — skip.
