About

HR deserves better than spreadsheets

Go HRM was born from a simple insight: an SME that runs invoicing, inventory or CRM in a modern tool… still runs payroll in Excel. We built the missing tool.

Our story

The problem

Legacy HR systems are built for large groups: heavy, expensive, slow to deploy. SMEs were left between spreadsheets, email threads and isolated tools — with the errors, re-keying and HR hours that implies.

The answer

Go HRM: a complete HR suite designed for teams of 10 to 5,000. Simple on the surface, serious underneath: EU hosting, GDPR compliance, API, and deployment measured in days, not months.

Our values

🧭

Simple first

If a module takes more than three clicks, it's not good. Every screen is designed to be usable without training.

🔒

Serious about data

HR data is sensitive. EU hosting, encryption, audit trail: security is a requirement, not an option.

🤝

Close to our customers

Support in French and English, 24-hour responses, and product changes directly inspired by your feedback.

Who is it for?

SMEs, 10–200 people

You want to stop the Excel + leave-tracker + isolated-payroll-tool patchwork. Go HRM brings it all together, with no integration project.

Fast-growing companies

Multi-site, multiple entities, complex payroll flows: the Scale plan with SSO and API lets your HR grow with the company.

Consultancies & outsourced HR

Manage multiple clients from the same workspace, with strict per-client permissions and consolidated reporting.
