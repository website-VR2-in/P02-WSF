FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is pricing calculated if I only have 30 employees?

Pricing is a flat per-active-user fee. 30 employees × €29 = €870/month on annual (Starter). You only pay for active accounts, never for unused seats.

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes. Plan changes are immediate and the price is adjusted pro-rata. No penalties, no minimum commitment period.

Are there onboarding or migration fees?

No. Assisted migration (headcount, leave, payroll history import) is included in the Growth plan and above.

What happens at the end of the 14-day trial?

Nothing. Your workspace is kept for 30 days, your data intact. You pick a plan when you want — no credit card required up front.
