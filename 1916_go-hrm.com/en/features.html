Features

Every module, in detail

Six modules that share the same employee base. Turn them on one by one, in the order that suits you.

👥 Headcount & employee records

Your single headcount database, always up to date.

Complete records

Identity, contract, role, pay, documents (contracts, certificates, clearances) with expiry alerts.

History

Transfers, promotions, pay changes: every change is tracked and timestamped.

Org chart

Automatically generated company hierarchy, exportable to PDF.

Import / export

Guided CSV on the way in, full export on the way out. Your data belongs to you.

HR alerts

Contract end dates, anniversaries, key dates: nothing slips past your HR calendar.

Permissions

Granular role-based access: each manager sees only their team.

🗓️ Time & leave

The most-used module of the day — designed to be as simple as possible.

Flexible time tracking

Web, mobile or badge: pick the tracking mode that fits each team.

Leave in one click

Request, approval, notification: the full cycle with no email in between.

Real-time balances

Every employee sees their entitlements and updated balances instantly.

Shift planning

Teams, on-call rotations, shared weekly schedules viewable by everyone.

Public holidays

Country presets (France and Europe) and handling of atypical holiday calendars.

Overtime

Counting, time-off compensation (TOIL) or premium pay: your rules, applied automatically.

💶 Payroll

A guided payroll run — no surprises, no re-keying.

Guided runs

Clear steps, automatic checks, validation at every milestone.

PDF payslips

Templates branded with your company, sent in one click.

Social declarations

Generate social declarations (DSN and equivalents) from the locked payroll.

Simulations

Impact of a raise, a departure, a hiring: simulate before you decide.

Pay history

Every payslip archived and viewable by the employee, with full traceability.

Accounting export

Export accounting entries to your management tool.

🎯 Recruitment

From job posting to onboarding, without changing tools.

Job postings

Post, track and close openings from a single interface.

Candidate pipeline

Drag-and-drop stages, scoring and shared notes.

Scheduled interviews

Shared calendar, candidate invitations, recruiter summaries.

Auto onboarding

Once the offer is accepted: account created, documents to sign, day-one checklist.

Employee record created

The hired candidate becomes an employee: zero re-keying, everything linked.

Dashboard

Conversion rates, time-to-hire, cost per role: HR data at a glance.

📈 Performance

Reviews that take an hour, not a day.

Goals

Set, track and evaluate individual and team goals.

Annual reviews

Structured forms, ratings, signed write-ups: everything archived.

360°

Multi-source feedback (peers, managers, clients) in an anonymous way.

Action plans

Turn reviews into concrete plans, tracked over time.

Skills

Skills framework, talent matrix, internal mapping.

Teams

Manager view of team goals and dynamics, on one screen.

⚙️ Platform & integrations

Solid technical foundations to grow with confidence.

REST API

Access your data and automate your workflows (payroll, headcount, leave).

Integrations

Accounting, badge systems, legacy HR, Slack / Teams: the connectors that matter.

Security

EU hosting

Data hosted in the European Union, DPA available.

Multi-site

Manage multiple entities or locations from the same workspace.

Support

Support in French and English, 24-hour response, deployment onboarding included.
