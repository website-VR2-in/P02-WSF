Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026. This page describes what go-hrm.com collects, how, and what your rights are.

1. Data controller

Go HRM ("we") is the controller for data collected through this website. Contact: hello@go-hrm.com.

2. Data collected

Browsing data: IP address (anonymised), browser type, pages viewed — via Google Analytics, only if you consent via the cookie banner.
Contact data: name, email, company, team size and message sent via the contact form. The form opens your mail app (mailto): nothing is stored on the site itself.
Technical cookies: a consent-preference cookie (ghrm_cookie_consent) is stored locally so we do not ask again.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Audience measurement (consent) and handling of commercial requests (pre-contractual measure). No data is used for targeted advertising or sold to third parties.

4. Hosting and location

The site is hosted via Vercel (infrastructure in the EU / USA). Go HRM product data (employees, payroll, leave) is hosted exclusively within the European Union, with a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) available on request.

5. Retention periods

Audience logs: up to 14 months (Google Analytics, anonymised IP). Contact data: 12 months after the last interaction, unless a legal obligation applies.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Exercise them at hello@go-hrm.com; we respond within 30 days. You may also contact the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics (ID G-XXXXXXXXXX, anonymised IP) is loaded only if you click "Accept" on the consent banner. "Decline" does not block any use of the site. Your choice is kept in localStorage under the key ghrm_cookie_consent.

8. Security

HTTPS across the site, security headers (CSP, HSTS via Vercel), encryption at rest. We update this policy as things evolve; the date at the top of the page is the last revision.

Questions about your data?

Write to us — we respond within 30 days.
