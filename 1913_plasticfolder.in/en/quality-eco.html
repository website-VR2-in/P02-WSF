Why we print on recycled polypropylene

Why a plastic folder is not a pollutant

It's not the plastic itself that pollutes, but the habits of inconsiderate people who throw plastic items in nature — so-called "disposable" objects like bags, bottles, cups and wrappers, designed for single use. The pollution question revolves more around the utility and durability of the item, and consumer habits, than its composition.

We use recycled polypropylene to produce our personalized pouches, and our inks are free of mineral or vegetable oils — compliant with French law n° 2020-105 (AGEC) and decree n° 2020-1725 (MOAH/MOSH limits of 1% and 0.1% for 2024/2025). Recycling PP saves raw materials by turning waste into reusable raw material: the products obtained are of high quality and recyclable again.

Offset printing with UV curing on recycled PP keeps the recycling cycle clean — a durable, reusable object is the opposite of the single-use model that pollutes.
