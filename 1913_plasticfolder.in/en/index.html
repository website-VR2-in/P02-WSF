Printed like paper, made of plastic

CMYK + white, 300 dpi, with the same quality as paper printing — plus the flexibility, durability and transparency that polypropylene adds. Matte or glossy finish as standard.

Standard or made to measure

Classic formats — open, closed, with flaps, letter format, tab closure, button, velcro — or a fully custom die. Tell us the reference (C01, G00, H01…) or your project.

Special inks & effects

Pantone colors, hot metallic inks (gold, silver…), 3D flip/flop lenticular effect, selective UV varnish — our inks are free of mineral and vegetable oils (AGEC-compliant).

Design & creation

No artwork? Our team designs it for you — basic or advanced design packages, or from your original creation. Templates are downloadable, video tutorials included.

Recycled & recyclable

We use recycled polypropylene; one tonne of recycled PP saves up to 830 L of oil. Your folder is neutral, easy to sanitize, and recyclable many times over its lifetime.
