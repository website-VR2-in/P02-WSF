# Social plan — plasticfolder.in

B2B packaging/print niche. Channels ranked by fit.

## Channel choice (WSF step 6)

| Channel | Fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (primary) | ★★★★★ | B2B buyers (agencies, brands, medical/legal orgs); case-study + eco-angle performs |
| **Instagram** | ★★★★ | Visual product (CMYK on plastic, transparency finishes, lenticular 3D); reels of print process |
| **X/Twitter** | ★★ | Low fit; use only for auto-posting syndication |
| **YouTube** | ★★★ | Tutorials/videos (live site already has a videos section) — repurpose |
| Facebook | ★★ | Secondary; some European SMBs |

## Content pillars

1. **Product beauty** — macro shots of CMYK print on PP, matte vs glossy, selective UV, metallic inks
2. **Process** — die-cutting, welding, lenticular, polybagging (builds trust + "made in Chennai" story)
3. **Eco/AGEC** — recycled PP, oil-free inks, "1 tonne saves 830 L oil" — strong for EU buyers
4. **Clients/case studies** — Fujifilm, TOEI Animation, Messer Medical, Ordre des Notaires
5. **Formats/education** — "C01 vs D01", "which template for 100+ pages", anti-RFID explained

## Cadence

- LinkedIn: 2/week (1 case-study, 1 process/eco)
- Instagram: 3/week (2 reels, 1 carousel)
- YouTube: 1/month (repurpose live-site tutorials)
- X: auto-syndicate top LinkedIn posts (see agents.md)
