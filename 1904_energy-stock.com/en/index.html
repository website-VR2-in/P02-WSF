Our Method

Consulting

With the support of HCEA Consulting, a company managed by the same CEO, we benefit from more than 15 years of experience in consulting and green energy optimization. Teams based in Asia and Europe multiply our efficiency.

✏️

Design

In 2015 our CEO launched a motto: "Look to the sky to produce your energy, look to the earth to store your energy". In 2021, the growth of electric batteries pushed us to create ESA. Just like Yuka, we want to positively influence public opinion on renewable energy storage.

⚡

Development

Strong experience in developing and certifying electrical products: in 2008, our CEO realized and had approved a "building-integrated" solar panel installation. Around the brand LUXCOMB, many solar solutions were developed, and an investment fund for new technologies was set up.
