2 · Energy Stock Alliance — energy storage sourcing

We have specialized in energy storage sourcing for European countries, with two teams: one based in Europe, one in Asia.

The challenge we take on

Shortage risk

Energy shortages — or the risk of them — force industry to invest in storage and peak absorption. Winter 2023 reminded us: entire sectors may shut down for hours or days to prioritize critical ones.

Lead-time & price risk

Months, even quarters, pass between a quote and the purchase of storage units. By signature day, lead times, prices and availability have changed — and any supply change is tedious and expensive, potentially impacting the whole project.

How we work

  1. 1

    We estimate your needs and select products and producers, drawing on expertise built since 2008 in engineering and OEM development for photovoltaic and LED.

  2. 2
