About

A productivity app that doesn't watch you.

GoMy was born from a simple observation: the tools that organize our days accumulate more and more reasons to watch us. We built the opposite.

Our story

The problem

Every “all-in-one” productivity app adds notifications, recommendations, “insights” — and behind it all, a profile being built about you. The result: you plan better, but you are observed more. The big tools target teams and companies; the person running their own life (studies, freelance, family, personal projects) always ends up on a tool built for someone else.

Our answer

GoMy is a personal command center: designed for one person first, hosted in Europe, ad-free, profiling-free, with no “feed” keeping you around. Just your tasks, your week, your goals, your habits — clear, fast, yours. The team is small, independent, and the product is funded by subscriptions, not by your data.

What we believe

🧭

Clarity first

A productivity tool that is itself confusing has failed. Every GoMy screen answers one question. The rest is one click away.

🔒

Your data is yours

EU hosting, encryption, one-click export, permanent deletion. If you leave, your data leaves with you — no hostage-taking.

🤝

Independence

GoMy is funded by its subscribers, not by advertisers or an acquisition. Product decisions are made for our users, not for a board.

Who GoMy is for

🎓 Students & recent grads

Exams, thesis, internships, life: it all fits in one week view. The Free plan comfortably covers a university year.

💼 Freelancers & independents

Clients, deliverables, pending invoices, routines: GoMy structures a one-person business without the tools of a ten-person one.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parents & multi-hats

Family life is project management without the budget. The shared family view and habits help you keep nothing on the floor.

🏃 People in transition

Job change, relocation, life project: quarterly goals and the week review give structure when everything is changing.
