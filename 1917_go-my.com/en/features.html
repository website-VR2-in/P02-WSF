Features

Every module serves one goal: make the week move.

No superfluous features. Each GoMy module answers one precise question: what to do, when, and whether it is paying off.

Tasks & projects

The fastest-to-use module in your arsenal. Capture in 3 seconds, hierarchy in 1 gesture.

⚡ Instant capture

Capture bar everywhere (web, mobile, widget). Raw text — GoMy detects the date, duration and project.

🗂️ Projects & lists

Projects with owners, deadlines and list, kanban or timeline views. Your favorite lists, always at hand.

🧩 Subtasks & dependencies

Decompose without drowning. Visual dependencies show what blocks what — without making a drama of it.

Weekly planning

GoMy's signature view: your week in one glance, with priorities already set.

📍 Daily focus

3–5 tasks that “really move things”. The rest waits politely in the list.

⏳ Time-blocking

Drag tasks into time blocks. GoMy protects your blocks and flags conflicts gently.

📊 Workload balance

Above the week: over-loaded days are visible from a distance. Rebalancing takes 30 seconds.

Goals & habits

Progress is measurable. Quarterly goals, daily habits — everything converges into a single view.

🎯 Quarterly goals

One goal, its metrics, its linked actions. Progress is computed automatically from your completed tasks.

🔁 Habits & streaks

Habit grid with streaks, weekly targets and reminders at your chosen time. Misses are visible, never shameful.

📈 Week review

On Friday, GoMy summarizes: done, postponed, habit progress, goals moved forward. 2 minutes to learn from the week.

Notes, capture & calendar

The connectivity bricks: everything that enters GoMy stays findable, and your calendar stays your source of truth.

📝 Linked notes

Every task, project or habit can carry notes. Full-text search over everything, even old weeks.

🔔 Smart reminders

Reminders at a fixed time, on arrival at a location, or “if the task is not done when expected”. You choose.

📅 Google, Outlook, ICS

Two-way with your calendar: events appear in your GoMy week, and time-blocks can create events.
