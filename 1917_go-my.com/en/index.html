Privacy

Your privacy, taken seriously

A productivity app knows your life: it is not acceptable that it exposes it. GoMy is designed GDPR-first, from the first line of code.

Infrastructure exclusively in the European Union. No data resale, no profiling, no third parties “optimizing” your attention.
