Privacy

Your data, our rules.

This page explains what data GoMy collects, why, for how long — and what you can do with it. Last updated: November 11, 2026.

1. Controller

The controller is GoMy, reachable at hello@go-my.com. (The exact legal entity will be published here at site launch; the contact details above are final.)

2. Data we process

Data Purpose Legal basis Retention
Account (email, hashed password) Authentication, sync Contract performance lifetime of account + 30 days
User content (tasks, projects, notes, habits) Requested service Contract performance lifetime of account; export then deletion
Usage events (pages viewed, features used) Product improvement Legitimate interest 14 months, aggregated
Audience measurement (Google Analytics, anonymized IP) Visit counting Consent 14 months (GA standard)
Contact emails Reply to your requests Necessary to respond 12 months

We do not sell or rent any data. We do not use targeted advertising or profiling.

3. Cookies & measurement

The site uses only technically necessary cookies. Google Analytics (audience measurement, anonymized IP) is loaded only if you accept it via the consent banner. The choice is stored locally in your browser under the gomy_cookie_consent key; you can remove it at any time (clear site data or decline again).

4. Hosting & transfers

All production data is hosted in the European Union. No user data is transferred outside the EU for processing. Any third-party services (billing) only process payment information, encrypted, and never receive your content.

5. Security

TLS encryption in transit, encryption at rest for databases, restricted and logged access, encrypted daily backups, annual security review. In the event of a significant incident, you will be informed without undue delay.

6. Your GDPR rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability (one-click JSON/CSV export in the app), and withdrawal of consent. To exercise a right: write to hello@go-my.com. Reply within 30 days. You may also contact the CNIL (or your local supervisory authority) at any time.

7. Minors

GoMy is intended for people aged 16 and over. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us: we will delete the account and its data.

8. Changes to this policy

Significant changes are announced in the app and by email, 30 days before they take effect. The date at the top of this page always indicates the version in force.
