How an engagement runs

Four steps. You always know what's next and what it costs.

  1. Audit (1–2 weeks)

    We map your workflows, data and tools, then hand you a prioritized roadmap with effort/ROI per item. Decision point: pick what to build.

  2. Build (2–6 weeks)

    We design, build and test the first agent in a sandbox, then connect it to your real tools. You watch it work on real cases before it goes live.

  3. Train (1 week, ongoing)

    Your team learns to prompt, review and improve it. We document everything in your language, in your tools.

  4. Scale (monthly)

    We measure against the baseline from the audit, fix what drifts, and queue the next build. You own the system; we stay on call.

FAQ

We're not technical. Can we still do this?

That's most of our clients. We work with whoever runs the process — a sales lead, an ops manager, an owner. Technical decisions are ours; you make business decisions.

What about our data and privacy?

Agents run on EU infrastructure where possible, with per-client isolation. We sign a DPA, map every data flow in the audit, and never use your data to build anything for someone else.

What happens after you leave?

You own everything: prompts, workflows, credentials, documentation. The system is designed to be operated by your team — that's the point of the training step.

How fast will we see a result?

First live agent in 2–6 weeks depending on scope. The audit tells you which use case pays back first — support triage and sales outreach typically show ROI within the first month of live running.
