# 1909 clawexperts.ai — social channel plan (step 6)

## Recommended channels (priority)
1. **LinkedIn (founder-first)** — services businesses are bought on the person behind them. Founder profile > company page for the first 3 months.
2. **YouTube** — build-in-public: screen recordings of real agent builds (anonymized) are the proof.
3. **X / Twitter** — AI builder community; secondary.

## First 30 days
- Day 1: launch post ("We build AI agents for companies that don't have an AI team").
- Day 4: "What we found in the first 10 AI audits" (even if it's 2 audits — pattern post).
- Day 8: screen-recording: support-triage agent handling 20 real tickets, annotated.
- Day 14: FR post: "Combien ça coûte vraiment d'avoir un département IA ?"
- Day 21: "The workflow we'd automate first in your business" — interactive, comment-driven.
- Day 30: first case study (or "what the first build taught us").

## KPIs
- LinkedIn: 300 followers month 1, 5 qualified conversations/week.
- YouTube: 3 build videos in month 1.
- Site: inbound "audit" requests ≥ 10/month by month 3.

## Tone
- Builder's voice: screenshots, numbers, "here's what broke and how we fixed it." No guru energy.
