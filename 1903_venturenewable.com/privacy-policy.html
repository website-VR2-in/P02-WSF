Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

VREW (Ventura Renewable Pvt Ltd) ("VREW", "we") provides solar and battery Energy-as-a-Service in India. We are committed to protecting the personal data of visitors, customers and investors.

2. Data we collect

• Contact details you share through the website form or email (name, email, phone, property or investment details).
• Usage data collected by Google Analytics 4 (pages visited, device type, approximate location, referrer). We do not use advertising cookies or sell personal data to third parties.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to respond to enquiries, design and deliver solar solutions, manage service contracts and investor relationships, and understand how the website is used so we can improve it.

4. Legal basis

We process personal data on the basis of consent, contract performance and legitimate interest, in line with applicable Indian data-protection law.

5. Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us operate (e.g., Google for analytics, in aggregated and pseudonymised form) or where required by law.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to contact@vrew.com.

Data queries: contact@vrew.com
