The VREW Model

Our model removes the main barrier to solar adoption: high upfront cost. We fully fund installation and maintenance of solar systems. In return, customers sign a 12-year service agreement with a fixed monthly payment — a reliable, long-term revenue stream for each project, secured by the physical asset and the service contract.

We target a stable, non-speculative 8% annual ROI. Returns are generated directly from the monthly payments of our diverse and growing customer base, giving this asset-backed model a lower-risk profile than traditional market investments.

VREW operates in full compliance with Indian regulations, including the Electricity Act, 2003. The model is structured as an Energy-as-a-Service (EaaS) / lease agreement — a legally robust framework — and leverages the 100% FDI allowance for renewable energy to welcome global partners.
