FAQ

Which Claw products are included?

All of them: sales (SalesClaw), security (SecureClaw), services (Claw Experts), the tool (ClawTool) and deployment (DeployMyClaw). You license what your teams actually use, under one contract and one audit stream.

Can we run it in our own cloud?

On the Enterprise plan, yes — private cloud or on-prem, same control plane. The model layer is yours either way; the runtime is isolated per tenant even in our EU cloud.

How does the audit stream reach us?

Native export to your SIEM (S3-compatible or streaming), or a read-only viewer. Logs are immutable, WORM-compatible, and retention is your setting from 90 days to 7 years.

What happens to our data if we leave?

Full export in open formats within 30 days of notice, then deletion with a certificate. No hold, no hostage. That clause is in the contract, not in a slide.
