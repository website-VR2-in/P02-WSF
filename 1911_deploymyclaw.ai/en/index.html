FAQ

What counts as a "run"?

One run is one request to your agent that returns a response. Streaming, retries and internal model calls don't multiply your count — you pay for what your users get.

Who hosts the model?

You bring the model (any OpenAI-compatible endpoint or your own). DeployMyClaw hosts the agent runtime — the thing that takes requests, calls your model, and answers. Model spend stays on your provider bill unless you connect it through us.

Where does my data live?

In EU data centers, isolated per tenant. Request/response logs follow your retention setting (7/90 days or off). We never use your payloads for training.

Can I move away later?

Yes. Your agent code and config export at any time, and endpoints follow the OpenAI-compatible shape so swapping the host is a base-URL change.
