## Sector-page content angles (phase 1)

- **Notaires (lawyers):** durable case for deeds/acts, branding for the Ordre, sanitizable, anti-RFID for card protection
- **Sante (medical):** sanitizable PP, patient-record sleeves, compliance (AGEC oil-free inks), Messer Medical proof
- **Anti-RFID:** contactless-card protection, how it works (metalized layer), product line detail

## Recommended title/desc pattern (FR example)

- Title: `Pochettes plastiques personnalisées — impression CMJN sur polypropylène | PlasticFolder` (≤60 chars)
- Desc: differentiator + EUR price cue ("dès 0,8410 €/HT à 2000 ex") + CTA
