# 1908 secureclawai.com — social channel plan (step 6)

## Recommended channels (priority)
1. **LinkedIn (company + founder)** — CISOs, IT managers, and SMB owners deciding on security. Highest-trust channel for B2B security.
2. **X / Twitter** — #InfoSec community; product demos + threat write-ups.
3. **YouTube (later)** — threat walkthroughs + product tours.

## First 30 days
- Day 1: launch post ("We're building an AI security copilot for companies that can't hire a CISO") LinkedIn + X.
- Day 3: "The 3-week blindness problem" (why breaches go unnoticed) — value post.
- Day 7: demo GIF: phishing email → detection → plain-language alert → session locked.
- Day 14: NIS2 explainer (FR post → /fr/).
- Day 21: first customer's false-positive stats (with permission).
- Day 30: "What a breach actually costs a 50-person company" post.

## KPIs
- LinkedIn: 150 followers month 1, 3 qualified DMs/week.
- X: 250 followers month 1.
- Site: social-referral sessions ≥ 15% of non-organic by month 2.

## Tone
- Evidence over adjectives. Numbers, screenshots, "what we'd do differently." No fear-mongering.
