FAQ

Can the AI do something that breaks my business?

Not in the default configuration. Remediation starts in suggest mode: every action is proposed, you approve. Auto-fix can be enabled per action type (e.g. lock a suspicious session), with an explicit whitelist and a full undo log.

Do you see my data?

We process metadata and event streams to detect threats — not the content of your files. Email analysis inspects headers, sender behavior and attachment signatures, and message content only for phishing classification. Everything is stored in the EU, isolated per tenant.

What about false positives?

Every alert carries a confidence score and a one-click "false positive" that tunes the model. Teams typically see under 2 actionable alerts per day after the first two weeks of tuning.

We already have antivirus / MFA. What do I gain?

Those tools protect one layer. SecureClaw correlates across email, identity, endpoints and cloud to catch the sequences that slip through — the valid-account abuse, the chained low-and-slow access, the phishing that lands after a legitimate password reset.
